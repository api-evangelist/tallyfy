Getting into Anthropic Claude Connectors Directory is a self-serve submission Anthropic reviews, and acceptance itself is the verified status. Two things decide most submissions: every tool annotated with the right read-only or destructive hint, and a public privacy policy. Anthropic says a missing or incomplete privacy policy is an immediate rejection.
