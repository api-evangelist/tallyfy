Insurance runs on document-heavy intake and adjudication: underwriting submissions, FNOL, claims handoffs, renewals. That is where AI extraction and triage pay off. But the coverage call and the denial stay human, because the NAIC unfair-claims rules require a reasonable, accurate explanation for any denied claim, and an unowned automated denial cannot give one.
