Self-updating SOPs sound futuristic, but the fix is dull: stop treating maintenance as a separate job nobody gets to, and let running the process update it. A study of more than 3,000 GitHub projects found most carry an outdated reference at some point, because the doc and the work drift apart. Execution keeps an SOP alive, not a review calendar.
